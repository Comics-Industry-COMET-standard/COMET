# Contributing to the COMET Standard

Thank you for helping improve the COMET Standard. It is a *living* standard, and it gets better when the people who use it — publishers, distributors, retailers, and software developers — propose improvements.

You do **not** need to be a programmer to contribute. There are two ways to suggest a change.

## 1. Contact the COMET team

The simplest way: send us a message through the [contact form at cometstandard.com/contact](https://cometstandard.com/contact/). Tell us what you'd like to see changed and why — a real-world example is always helpful. The team will take it from there.

## 2. Open an issue

If you have a GitHub account, open an issue using one of our templates:

- **Propose a new field** — something the standard doesn't yet capture.
- **Change a picklist value** — add, rename, or clarify a controlled-vocabulary entry.
- **Report an error** — a wrong description, typo, or inconsistency.

Describe the *what* and the *why*. That's enough to start the conversation.

## What happens next

The Standards Committee reviews your suggestion (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)) and handles the technical implementation. A change is accepted only by **unanimous consent** of the Committee, and accepted changes are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Code of conduct

Be constructive and respectful. We're all here to make comics data better for the whole industry.
